Android Native Solutions

We execute at framework, HAL, and validation layers with deterministic delivery for OEMs, silicon vendors, and platform teams.

AOSP & Native Frameworks

We design and stabilize Android platform internals where correctness, performance, and maintainability must coexist. Typical engagements include Binder-heavy service orchestration, native daemon lifecycle control, JNI boundary hardening, and subsystem-level behavior tuning under strict release timelines.

  • Framework architecture and subsystem decomposition
  • Native process control and crash-recovery design
  • JNI contract hardening and performance profiling
  • Binder IPC interface strategy and reliability tuning

Media Frameworks Specialization

We optimize end-to-end media pipelines for low-latency and high-fidelity behavior across constrained and high-throughput environments. This includes AudioFlinger policy/routing control, AudioTrack buffer dynamics, camera/video path integration, and custom Audio HAL variants aligned to hardware constraints.

  • AudioFlinger and AudioTrack path optimization
  • Custom Audio HAL architecture and implementation
  • Low-latency DSP routing and offload strategy
  • Video and camera stack integration and validation

Interface Migrations

We lead large-scale migration programs where interface evolution impacts multiple product lines and partner teams. Our focus is low-risk transitions from HIDL to AIDL while preserving compatibility, limiting integration churn, and ensuring measurable platform stability through staged rollout validation.

  • HIDL-to-AIDL interface transformation strategy
  • Cross-team migration sequencing and risk isolation
  • Vendor compatibility and regression prevention
  • Platform bring-up for evolving hardware targets

Automation & Compliance

We build hardened validation pipelines for CTS, VTS, and GTS at scale, with reliable failure classification and tight feedback loops into platform engineering. Our optimizations have repeatedly delivered dramatic runtime reductions while preserving confidence in release-quality compliance signals.

  • CTS, VTS, and GTS automation architecture
  • Pipeline parallelization and runtime minimization
  • Flake classification and deterministic rerun policy
  • Release gating and compliance evidence packaging

Build Systems Modernization

We modernize legacy Android build surfaces by migrating historical Android.mk logic into hermetic Soong and Android.bp workflows. The goal is reproducibility, cleaner dependency graphs, and maintainable build governance that supports long-lived platform branches.

  • Android.mk auditing and migration planning
  • Soong and Android.bp refactoring and rollout
  • Hermetic build enforcement and dependency cleanup
  • CI build determinism and long-term maintainability
